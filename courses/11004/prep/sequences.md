# prep — sequences (2.1, p11–13)
1. One idea: a sequence is just a function whose inputs are 1,2,3,… ; two famous kinds are "add the same each step" (arithmetic) and "multiply by the same each step" (geometric); a third builds the next term from the previous ones (recursive).
2. Why: lists of numbers with a rule show up everywhere (savings, growth, counting ways); the point is to get the n-th term and the sum of n terms without writing all of them. Hook: sum 4,7,…,19 without adding one by one.
3. Chain: sequence = function on ℕ → a_n=f(n); Im(f) is the *set* of values (order and repeats lost) → arithmetic: constant difference d → a_n=a_1+(n-1)d because n-1 jumps → sum: pair first+last (every pair equal because one grows by d the other shrinks by d) → S_n=(a_1+a_n)/2·n; substitute a_n → (2a_1+d(n-1))/2·n. Geometric: constant ratio q → a_n=a_1 q^{n-1} (n-1 multiplications) → sum: S−qS telescopes: S(1−q)=a_1(1−q^n), divide needs q≠1 (q=1: S=n·a_1). Recursive: next term from previous ones; arithmetic/geometric are recursive too (a_{n+1}=a_n+d); Fibonacci a_{n+2}=a_n+a_{n+1}; stairs: last hop 1 step from n+1... or 2 steps.
4. Hard: (a) n vs n−1 in a_n (count jumps not terms); (b) q^n in the sum vs q^{n-1} in the term; (c) {a_n} as list vs Im(f) as set ((-1)^n has 2 values); (d) graph of sequence is dots not a curve; (e) sum needs n first (find n from last term); (f) q=1 breaks the geometric sum formula. Fixes: jump-count figures, trap quizzes, mistakes box.
5. Exam: a_n and S_n of arithmetic/geometric from two given terms (set up equations); find n from last term then sum; compute terms of a recursion; recognise type from first terms.
6. Central image: dots/bars of (n,a_n); arithmetic = equal vertical jumps; geometric = equal multiplications; sum of arithmetic = stack first+last bars into equal columns.
7. Emphasis: formulas for a_n and S_n of both (memorise), n−1 trap. One line: harmonic example, recursion def. Not saying: proof of Fibonacci closed form, limits.
8. Hard case: odd n in the pairing (middle term pairs with itself) — formula still true via average×count; not drawn (n=6 shown), said once in narration. Geometric q=1.
9. Intuitions: pairing (equal columns); S−qS cancels the middle (shifted copy). Condition q≠1 because we divide by 1−q.
